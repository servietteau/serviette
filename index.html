THE GUEST & FLOOR LOOP

Three frictionless steps. No apps or logins.

From the table scan to staff recognition, the feedback loop closes before the diner walks out.

Scan & Spin

Guests scan a branded QR stand, rate their waiter in 15 seconds, and unlock a gamified Spin-the-Wheel reward (free coffee, dessert, or 10% off).

Feedback logs automatically into Google Sheets and your executive console — highlighting table number, server name, order pace, and sentiment.
