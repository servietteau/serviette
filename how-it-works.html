HOW IT WORKS

From a guest's tap to a fix on the floor.

No extra work for guests, and no extra app for your team to check. Here's exactly what happens between a tap and a table getting saved.

01

Ask

A quick tap at checkout, mid-meal, or post-dinner shares how the visit is going. No app, no login, no printed card to fill out and forget.

02

See it live

Scores, comments and sentiment land on your dashboard the moment a guest taps — not bundled into a weekly report.

03

Act

An alert routes straight to whoever's on the floor right now — while there's still time to check on the table.
