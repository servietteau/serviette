QUESTIONS

Before you book a walkthrough.

Do guests need to download an app?

No. Guests tap a QR code or NFC tag at the table — there's nothing to download and nothing to log into.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans are billed monthly with no lock-in, and the first month is free on every tier.

Can it handle more than one venue?

Yes — Multi-Location View rolls every site into one dashboard, with role-based access so each venue only sees its own floor.
