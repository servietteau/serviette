Why we built this

A short story about walking out of a lot of restaurants disappointed, and never once saying why.

THE SHORT VERSION

It's not a people problem. It's a feedback gap.

The idea didn't start as a feedback platform. It started as a habit of leaving restaurants disappointed and never coming back — without ever telling anyone why. Good food, good room, but a wait at the bar that ran too long, or a question brushed off mid-sentence. There was never a natural moment to say something, so nothing got said.

Then came a trip overseas, where service felt instinctive — guests were read, not just served, and small things got corrected before they became complaints. That's when it clicked:

"It's not a people problem. It's a feedback gap."

By the time a bad night turns into a Google review, the moment to fix it has already passed. Serviette closes that gap — a guest scans a code at the table, and their mood becomes a live signal a manager can act on in minutes, not a review discovered three days later.
